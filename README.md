# Laboratory of Intelligent Systems - Selection Tasks

**Candidate:** Sanjana Laxman
**Laboratory:** Laboratory of Intelligent Systems, Kyushu University (Prof. Danilo Vasconcellos Vargas)

## Repository Contents
- **[B1] Image Reconstruction** (`B1_Image_Reconstruction/`): Convolutional Denoising Autoencoder (CDAE) on MNIST across Gaussian, Salt-and-Pepper, and Mixed noise regimes.
- **[B5] Dynamical Systems & Chaos Analysis** (`B5_Dynamical_Systems_AI/`): Bifurcation cascade, trajectory divergence, and Largest Lyapunov Exponent analysis of a 2-neuron coupled RNN.
- **[B6] Blind Signal Separation (BSS)** (`B6_BSS_Project/`): FastICA separation of 2-source and 3-source soundwave mixtures.
- **[A2] Continual General Chunking Problem and SyncMap** (`A2_Adversarial_Robustness/`): SymmetricalSyncMapMA evaluation and improvement across Overlap 1 and Overlap 2 benchmarks.
- **[A3]{X} Edge-Weighted Graph Clustering in SyncMap** (`A3_SyncMap_Graph_Clustering/`): Replacement of DBSCAN with an adaptive local-scaling, co-activation-weighted graph and Louvain modularity optimization.
